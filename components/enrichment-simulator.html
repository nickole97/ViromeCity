One sample, three protocols

The same population enters VLP enrichment, bulk shotgun metagenomics, and probe capture. Each gate removes particles by a physical property: diameter at the filter, buoyant density at the gradient, capsid protection at the nuclease, panel membership at hybridization. Some of the viruses here are not free particles at all but prophage and endogenous retroviruses riding inside a cell, which changes which gates can reach them. Watch what gets to sequencing.

Diameters and buoyant densities are nominal textbook values for each morphological group, used to drive the gate predicates. They are illustrative parameters, not measurements, and particle counts are chosen for legibility rather than to represent abundance. Nothing here is derived from HVP data. Edit the TAXA table in the source to change any value; the simulation reads it directly.

Gates: clarify pellets intact cells · 0.22 µm filter passes particles under the pore size · density gradient keeps a 1.26–1.52 g/mL band · nuclease degrades nucleic acid not inside a capsid or cell · probe hybridization keeps only panel members · a DNA-only library drops RNA genomes. Deterministic: fixed seed, so the same settings always give the same counts.
